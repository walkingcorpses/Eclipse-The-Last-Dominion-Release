# ECLIPSE: THE LAST DOMINION

*A single-player, dark-fantasy action RPG for PC, built in Unreal Engine 5.*

> **Status: early development (pre-production).** Nothing here is playable yet. This repository holds public news and development updates only. It contains no game code or assets.

---

## The world

The Crowned Empire tried to bind life, death and memory into a celestial mechanism called **the Meridian**. The experiment failed. The boundary between the living world and **the Hollow** fractured. Now the sun suffers black eclipses, the forgotten return in distorted forms, and memories drift loose from the people who owned them.

You carry a mark tied to the Meridian. Nobody knows yet whether it is a key, a wound, a warning, or proof that you were part of the catastrophe.

## What we are building

- **Single-player only.** A complete offline experience. No multiplayer and no always-online requirement.
- **First-person and third-person.** Switch freely at any time. Both are fully supported for exploration, combat, magic and interaction.
- **Eight connected regions:** Ashen Kingdom, Hollow Forest, Frostfall, Drowned Coast, Emberlands, Undercrown, Forgotten Reach and the Imperial Dominion.
- **A cinematic, voiced story.** Create your character, and the opening cinematic stars them. Your choices shape factions, companions, settlements and the world. The story has multiple endings.
- **Flexible combat.** Six disciplines (Knight, Berserker, Ranger, Mage, Assassin and Paladin) that you can mix into hybrid builds.
- **A living world.** Gathering, crafting and professions, farming and livestock, settlement building, trading, factions and diplomacy.
- **PC settings and accessibility treated as core features.** Full remapping, controller support, subtitle options and camera-motion controls.
- **Premium, one-time purchase.** No pay-to-win, no loot boxes, no energy timers and no mandatory microtransactions.
